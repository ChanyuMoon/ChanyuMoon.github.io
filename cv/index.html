Projects

  • 2025 - 2026
    Muscle-Guided Latent Representation Distillation for Spinal-Based Motor Intention Decoding

    Lead researcher (M.S. thesis research).

    • Developed an Electrospinogram (ESG)-based motor decoding framework to address the limitations of EMG/EEG-based decoding for neuroprosthetic control.
    • Designed ESG-CL (contrastive learning) and ESG-DT (domain translator), which use EMG-derived latent representations to decouple motor features from motor-sensory intertwined ESG signals.
    • Built the end-to-end cross-modal training pipeline, including opposite-limb and inter-subject scenarios and a simulated quadriplegia (zero-EMG) setting.
  • 2024 - 2025
    OFF-CLIP: Improving Normal Detection Performance on Chest X-ray

    Co-first author, MICCAI 2025.

    • Redefined the InfoNCE loss to treat all normal samples as positive pairs (off-diagonal term loss), preventing mis-clustering of semantically identical normal cases.
    • Implemented all baseline models and built the benchmarking environment for comparative analysis.
  • 2025 - 2025
    Efficient Biomedical Time-series Contrastive Learning

    Co-first author, ICTC 2025.

    • Formulated a first-order Taylor approximation of InfoNCE that bypasses the batch-by-batch similarity matrix.
    • Achieved up to 7.3x speedup and 8.5x peak-memory reduction on TS2Vec (UCI HAR) with comparable accuracy.

I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST, advised by Prof. Jong Chul Ye. I earned my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence (advised by Prof. Ji-Woong Choi) and B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, both from DGIST.

My research focuses on Medical AI, with a particular emphasis on Vision-Language Models (VLMs), Large Language Models, and Agentic AI. I am passionate about applying these advanced techniques to tackle critical challenges in the medical domain.
